## Overview

College turns into one long optimisation streak. Streakbreaker hands you small sidequests, you go do them, and the whole point is that other people are doing them too.

## What I built

A mobile-first app where the sidequests are shared rather than solo, so breaking your own optimisation streak becomes the feature instead of a failure. It is social first, less a habit tracker you grind alone, more a reason to look up.
