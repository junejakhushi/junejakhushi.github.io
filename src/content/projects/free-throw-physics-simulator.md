## Overview

A free-throw game that turns Newtonian mechanics into something you can feel. I started it in 11th grade because the physics textbook was putting me to sleep.

## What I built

A Raspberry Pi and an OpenCV pipeline track the shot in real time, score it, and drive a physics game around it, so the mechanics you are learning are the mechanics you are playing with.
