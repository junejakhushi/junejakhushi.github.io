## Overview

A two-box system: a gas-sensor sniffer that turns smells into numbers, and a valve-based player that releases blended scents.

## My role

Collaborator on a two-person build.

## Problem

Capture a smell as data and reproduce it, at least roughly, from a small set of scent components.

## What I built

A sniffer box of gas sensors that profiles a smell into a signal, and a player box of valves that blends stored scents to approximate it.

## Results / what I learned

In progress, with a demo planned for January 2027.
