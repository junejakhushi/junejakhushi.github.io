## Overview

The tooling behind the precalculus course I TA, aimed at catching misconceptions early and giving instructors a clearer view of where students are.

## What I built

Edpuzzle checkpoints and misconception probes layered onto the course videos, plus an instructor dashboard in Next.js and Supabase that pulls from Canvas and Gradescope. A course-grounded LLM tutor diagnoses missing prerequisites when a student is stuck.
