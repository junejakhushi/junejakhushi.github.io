## Overview

Waypoint Wars is a scavenger hunt you play with your feet. Checkpoints are real places you have to physically reach, and you prove you got there with a photo. The hunts themselves are generated by AI and written by other players.

## My role

Designer and builder.

## Problem

A scavenger hunt is only fun if the checkpoints are real, the difficulty is fair, and the whole thing feels authored. Generating that automatically, at the quality a person would expect, is harder than it sounds.

## What I built

Hunts are generated with Gemini and narrated with ElevenLabs, so each one arrives with a voice and a story instead of a bare list of coordinates. The community writes and remixes hunts for each other. Three problems make it interesting: verifying that a photo actually shows the checkpoint and not a lookalike down the street, generating hunts that are hard but still possible, and making a hunt a stranger wrote feel like someone designed it for you.

## Results / what I learned

Still building. Photo verification and difficulty tuning are the two problems I am spending the most time on, because they are what separate a real hunt from a random list of places.
