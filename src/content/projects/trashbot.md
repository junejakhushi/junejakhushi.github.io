## Overview

A robot on an RC base that picks up and deposits paper, beanbags, and 1L bottles, including into a moving bin.

## My role

Designer and builder, for a Robot Building Practices course.

## Problem

Grab a range of object sizes and weights reliably with one mechanism, and still land them in a bin that is moving.

## What I built

A single two-finger servo and gear claw on a motorized pivot arm, built from VEX tubing and designed in Onshape, mounted on an RC base.

## Results / what I learned

Still building. Keeping one claw versatile across paper, beanbags, and bottles is the whole design problem, and it is where most of the iteration goes.
